# Suniye landing product context

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the landing brief: standalone HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with a dependency-free Node build. No packages, accounts, providers or hosting are created.

## Users

Hindi-speaking parents with weak eyesight, and the family member helping them evaluate and set up an Android reading aid. The founder's mother and father use Redmi A4 phones; observed use on those phones remains pending.

## Product Purpose

Help parents hear small print from messages, pictures, paper and PDFs, with large Hindi controls and reachable Stop/Repeat. The landing page lets a visitor hear an existing synthetic example, see real native screens and find the public pilot and caregiver instructions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Android 11+ pilot. Bundled OCR supplies text; original text remains visible. New speech needs internet and an authenticated backend. Cached Raju audio supports repeat and slower playback.
- ElevenLabs Raju is speech, not reasoning. Optional Gemma explanations are experimental and separate from the original; hosted Gemma is unconfigured.
- The public linked download is 0.2.0-elevenlabs. The parent orchestrator selected a separate 0.1 APK for Appetize cloud-emulator checks; no family-use outcome is claimed. Local 0.3.0-parent-ux and newer backend fixes are not in that download.
- Actual WhatsApp use, physical Redmi performance, listening comfort and real Hindi voice recognition remain separate checks. Dense OCR, amounts, dates and medicine labels require human checking.
- No invented testimonials, family outcomes, general accuracy or accessibility certification.

## Brand Commitments

Hindi first; warm, accessible language; the app's existing cream and deep teal identity; real Android screenshots and recorded demos. No heavy 3D, remote fonts, generated imagery or provider requests.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, outputs/suniye/README.md and the supplied October 4 revised DEV article establish claims. Native captures and the dated Raju bill MP3 are copied unchanged from outputs/suniye/docs/evidence. Recordings use synthetic inputs and do not establish parent-phone usability.

## Product Principles

1. Make the parent's benefit understandable before technical details.
2. Give speech a deliberate start, an immediate Stop and clear failure recovery.
3. Keep the original and distinguish speech from generated explanation.
4. Keep release versions and evidence scope explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Large Hindi text, generous touch targets, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks and control labels. Readable static content without JavaScript; local audio with native fallback. Respect reduced motion and avoid autoplay. Full assistive-technology and parent usability audits are not claimed.
