High
H1. Signs are dropped for unit and currency numbers, which regresses a case the old code handled.
Where:
backend/src/hindi-speech.js:43-56
 ( CONTEXT  and  contextual ) and the Java twin  HindiSpeech.java:33-34 .
Cause:  The new pass runs before  TOKEN . Its lookbehind allows a preceding  - ,  −  or  + , so it consumes only the digits. The sign is left as a bare character and nothing reads it.
Probes (old to new):
बैलेंस -500 रुपये है : "माइनस पाँच सौ रुपये" became "-पाँच सौ रुपये".
−500 रुपये : "माइनस ..." became "−पाँच सौ रुपये".
छूट -10% है : "माइनस दस%" became "-दस प्रतिशत".
-5 mg : "माइनस पाँच mg" became "-पाँच मिलीग्राम".
+5%  became "+पाँच प्रतिशत".
Why it matters:  A debit or negative balance can be heard as a credit.
Fix:  Allow an optional sign in  CONTEXT , only when it is preceded by the start, whitespace or  ( :  (?:(?<=^|[\s(])[+−-])? . Then pass it to  numeric() , which already says "माइनस" or "प्लस". Keep  5-10 mg  as a range, which currently reads "पाँच-दस मिलीग्राम" and is fine. Change both the JS and Java copies, and add fixtures for each case above, since the current fixtures miss signed units.
H2. A single Mongo error marks the whole store down for 30 seconds, including expected ones.
Where: resilient-store.js:16 . Any thrown error sets  ready=false  and  lastAttempt=now , and  connect()  refuses to retry until  retryMs  (30 s) passes.
Probe:  A fake  updateOne  that throws  {code:11000}  left  available()  false. The next  findOne  threw  STORE_UNAVAILABLE .
Reachable path: backboard.js:12  deliberately tolerates a duplicate-key error on the first quota upsert of the day. Two concurrent first calls trigger it.  resilientStore  then flags the store down, and the very next  findOneAndUpdate  in  reserve()  throws  STORE_UNAVAILABLE . That one request fails and settings sync and Gemma stay down until the retry window passes. The race is rare, but any application-level Mongo error does the same.
Fix:  Flag unavailable only for network, selection and timeout errors ( MongoNetworkError ,  MongoNetworkTimeoutError ,  MongoServerSelectionError ,  MongoNotConnectedError ,  MongoPoolClearedError ). Rethrow everything else untouched.
Related, low: STORE_UNAVAILABLE  is not a  PublicError , so  server.js  returns a generic 500  READ_FAILED . Android maps that to "check your internet" for the caregiver summary and settings calls. Add a  SYNC_UNAVAILABLE  503 mapping.

M2. Rate-limit design holds, but two limits remain.
Confirmed by probe:
70 unauthenticated  /health  hits from one IP gave 60 × 200 then 10 × 429.
70 bad-token requests gave 60 × 401 then 10 × 429.
A valid-token request straight afterwards passed and kept its own counter.
70 more authenticated requests gave exactly 12 × 429 (the 60/min family bucket).
Limit 1: server.js  has no  trustProxy . Behind Render,  request.ip  is probably the proxy address, so every public visitor to the landing page,  /health  and the static files shares one 60/min bucket. A judge could get a 429 on the landing page after about 60 hits a minute from anyone. I could not check this live. Setting  trustProxy  to the correct hop count fixes it, but verify the real client-IP header on Render first.
Limit 2:  The authenticated bucket is one shared key for all devices and routes. Three parents plus the caregiver page share 60/min. That is fine for the demo and worth stating.
Side effect:  The 401 reply is sent from the app-level hook, before the route-level limiter, so the explicit  unauthenticatedLimit  is what counts bad tokens. That works as intended.
